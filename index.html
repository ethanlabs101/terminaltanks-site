SAVE DATA

    
Your progress — tournament levels, unlocks, loadouts, settings and anything the game stores — is saved on this device, in this browser, using the browser's built-in IndexedDB storage. There is no account and no server: nothing is uploaded, and every player on every device has their own separate save.

    

    
KEEP IT SAFE

    
    
      
  • Clearing site data or using a private window deletes the save. Safari may also clear it after a week without a visit.
    • 
      
  • To move to another browser or device, Export a backup file and Import it there.

HOW TO PLAY

    
KEYBOARD

    
A/D or ←→ aim · W/S or ↑↓ power · Shift bigger steps · Space fire · Enter select · Esc pause / back · H switch home screen.

    
TOUCH

    
Phones and tablets get an on-screen pad (toggle it with Touch pad). SHIFT makes arrows take big steps, and KEY opens the keyboard for typing. Landscape gives the biggest battlefield.

    
GAMEPAD

    
Plug in or pair a controller and press a button: D-pad / stick aim, A fire / select, B back, X enter, LB hold for big steps.

    
GOOD TO KNOW

    
    
      
  • The game needs a terminal of at least 96×28 — the page picks a font size to fit; use A− / A+ to tune.
    • 
      
  • The first visit downloads the Python runtime (about 10 MB). After that it loads from cache.
    • 
      
  • Turn the game's SOUND setting to BELL for impact sounds.
